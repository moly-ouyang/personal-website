About

Interdisciplinary media and cultural research

I am a Master's student majoring in Management and minoring in Financial Media at Peking University HSBC Business School. My work brings together questions from media studies, cultural sociology, and digital platform research.

Short Bio

I am interested in how popular culture becomes socially meaningful once it circulates through platforms, fan communities, and mediated markets. Across topics such as games, online fandoms, interactive media, and platformed cultural consumption, I ask how identities are formed through taste, attachment, and participation.

A related strand of my research explores AI-mediated interaction: how conversational systems, recommendation environments, and generative tools reshape expectations of presence, agency, and intimacy in everyday digital life. Much of this curiosity is grounded in the study of contemporary China, where questions of aspiration, governance, and mediated sociality often become especially vivid.

Research Approach

I am most interested in work that connects close cultural interpretation with questions of infrastructure, circulation, and social meaning. This means reading media objects carefully while also paying attention to the platform logics and communities that organize them.

Research

Current research themes

My current interests are connected by a shared concern with how media environments shape visibility, belonging, interpretation, and everyday forms of social interaction.

Theme 01

Platform Cultures and Visibility

I study how recommendation systems, interface conventions, and platform metrics shape what becomes visible, shareable, and culturally valuable in contemporary digital environments.

Theme 02

Subculture, Taste, and Distinction

I am interested in how communities produce belonging through aesthetic judgment, niche references, and hierarchies of taste, especially where fandom, lifestyle, and consumption overlap.

Theme 03

Games and Interactive Media

Games and adjacent media matter to me as sites where narrative, systems, play, and affect come together. I approach them as cultural forms that structure experience as much as representation.

Theme 04

AI-mediated Interaction

I am also exploring how chatbots, generative systems, and other AI interfaces enter everyday life as social presences, reshaping norms of communication, authorship, and attachment.

Context

Across these themes, I am especially interested in contemporary China as a setting where platform governance, mediated aspiration, and popular-cultural experimentation meet in highly visible ways.
